What Relay is

Relay is a campus incident relay. Anyone on campus — a student walking past a loose stairwell railing, a warden noticing a gas smell near the mess kitchen — can file a report in under a minute. Relay reads the urgency and the department, then puts that report directly on the desk of the person who can actually act on it. No group chats, no forwarded emails, no report quietly dying in an inbox.

How a report travels

01

Capture

Snap a photo with the built-in camera or pick one from your gallery, add a short title, a location and a category. Detail helps, but a title alone is enough to get moving.

02

Route

Urgency decides the path. Low and medium reports land with the department HOD. High reports go to the Vice Principal and the HOD together. Critical reports reach the Principal and Vice Principal at the same moment, so nothing waits on one person being free.

03

Resolve

The receiving desk acknowledges, moves the report into progress and finally marks it resolved. Every step is stamped on a timeline the reporter can see, so "we're looking into it" becomes something you can actually check.

Who sees what

Relay gives each role its own workspace instead of one crowded dashboard.

  • Students file reports and track their own history, with live status on every ticket they raised.
  • HODs get an ops board scoped to their department, sorted with the most urgent report first.
  • Vice Principal sees everything escalated as high or critical across all departments.
  • Principal sees the critical tier — the handful of things that genuinely cannot wait until tomorrow.
  • Admin gets the full portal: campus-wide statistics, search and filters across every report, and the directory of registered accounts.

Signing up

Pick the role that matches your desk on the sign-up tab. Every role stays selectable, including Admin — if a desk already has a holder, Relay simply notes who that is next to the option rather than locking you out. Prefer to look around first?
